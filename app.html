Everything here is editable: names, schedules, treatment type, order, and which optional steps are included. The arrows on each row move a step earlier or later. How to use the builder

Cycle length and count set each step's duration. "Runs alongside the previous step" draws a step above the timeline instead of after it (radiation during immunotherapy, for example).
